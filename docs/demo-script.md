# FlareIt Demo Script

For the complete production timeline, shot list, overlays, and copy-ready English TTS script, use [VIDEO_DEMO_GUIDE.md](VIDEO_DEMO_GUIDE.md). This file remains the compact operator checklist.

Target duration: 3 minutes. Record the same flow three times before submission.

## 1. Direct Mint (60 seconds)

1. Open `/onboarding` with the recipient wallet on Coston2.
2. Prepare a direct mint and show the live Core Vault, gross amount, fees, net estimate, and memo.
3. Sign the XRPL Testnet payment in Xaman and show FlareIt receive the signing result.
4. Open the automatically populated XRPL transaction link.
5. Refresh until the FTestXRP balance increase is visible.

Do not continue if the XRPL destination, memo, or amount differs from the prepared payload.

## 2. Send And Receive (75 seconds)

1. Open `/send` with Wallet A.
2. Approve FTestXRP and show the confirmed approval receipt.
3. Send to Wallet B and show the separate confirmed send receipt.
4. Switch to `/dashboard` with Wallet B or search Wallet B.
5. Show the indexed event, amount, block, and explorer link.

## 3. Merchant QR Payment (35 seconds)

1. Open `/merchant`, enter a merchant Coston2 address and IDR quote.
2. Generate the five-minute invoice and state: "This is a non-custodial FXRP payment; a licensed PJP is the future IDR adapter."
3. Approve and pay the merchant.
4. Show the payment receipt, deadline, and merchant balance change.

## 4. Close (10 seconds)

State the boundary clearly: direct mint and FXRP movement use Flare/FAssets now; production IDR/QRIS settlement requires a licensed PJP integration and is the next commercial milestone.
